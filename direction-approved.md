## What that decides

**Chosen:** Direction 2, The Yantra — with a correction that overrides the draft as presented.

The draft drew a full Kali Yantra: square enclosure (bhūpura) with four gates, eight-petalled lotus,
circle, triangle, bindu. **The square, the lotus, the circle and the four gates are rejected.** The yantra
is the triangle and the bindu — nothing else. That matches the site's existing mark (nested trikona + bindu)
and the śakti trikoṇa at the centre of the traditional construction.

**Consequences accepted:**
- No bhūpura gates: the four-gate navigation is gone. Navigation becomes the three triangles plus the bindu.
- The yantra is one downward triangle (apex down) drawn as three nested outlines — the three ways in — and a
  bindu at the centroid.
- Kali is the centre; Adya Ma is the outer triangle (the source); Bhairava the middle (the gate) — the
  descent the archive itself describes.
- Mahakala no longer has a ring of its own. It stays as a content hub but is read inside Bhairava
  (`Mahakala and KalaBhairava — Simplified` is already a single piece). **Needs the author's confirmation.**

Still open, asked and unanswered: the deity taxonomy (derived, not confirmed) and whether Devanagari verse
text will be supplied.
