Sun guides the way,
Darkness fades away.
Heart finds its flame
No chains can tame.

Let go of old debts,
Let love be your net.
In stillness, you see:
The soul is free.

No words, no need
Just light, just seed.
The path is inside,
Where peace resides.

Jai Kalabhairava Baba

Jai Ma Adya
