The site has moved. Same writing, same doors, new house.

The shape is the one we chose: three nested triangles, pointing down, and a bindu at the centre. Adya Ma is the outer triangle, Bhairava the gate, Kali the centre. The bindu is the smallest thing on the page and the one that holds the newest piece. For this opening it holds her face from the Tandava cover, the same image that introduced the Shri Kali Tandava Stotram.

Nothing was thrown away. All thirty-two old URLs still open exactly where they opened before. The poems are still poems, the stotrams still carry verse, translation and commentary, and the archive still holds every piece in the same order. This note is the thirty-third.

What changed is the way in. The pages are lighter, the images are sized for the screen instead of arriving at full weight, and every poem, stotram and article now has a share card. The reading page gives the stotram its own axis: verse, translation, commentary, and a margin that tells you where you are.

A few things are still being carried in. The Devanagari verse is not yet in the archive; it will come from the source texts, not from memory. The image-only post waits behind a quiet holding page. And the older images still carry the marks of the hands that made them.

Jai Ma Adya. Jai Kalabhairava.
