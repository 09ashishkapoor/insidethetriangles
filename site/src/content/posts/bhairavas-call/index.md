Fearless Bhairava, dark and bright,
Shiva’s anger, Shiva’s light.
Dog beside you, fierce and true,
You protect the ones who kneel to you.

You cut the ego, proud and vain,
Yet you heal the deepest pain.
With Kaali’s love, you dance so free,
You are the path, the key to see.

Chant your name, just one time,
Feel your power, wild, divine.
No big prayers, no gold, no show
Just a heart that wants to know.

Bhairava, take my hand,
Burn my sins in firebrand.
Make me strong, make me wise,
Open wide my sleeping eyes.

Jai Bhairava
