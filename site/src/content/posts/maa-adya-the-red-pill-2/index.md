Adya Ma's fiery gaze comes down
A transformative energy that knows no bound
She cuts through my ego's tangled threads
revealing thy true self, buried deep in unknown dread
With each step, the haziness goes away
Exposing the true darkness, black and grey
The worldly attachments shatter like glass
As Adya Ma's truth begins to amass
In her dark embrace, the heart unfolds
A labyrinth of self-doubt, where shadows grow old
Through trials and tribulations, she guides
Until the heart is remade, and Moksha abides
Transmuted, I emerge, reborn anew
A lotus unfurled, with heart pure and true.
